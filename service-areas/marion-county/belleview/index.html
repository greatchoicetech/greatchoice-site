This city

Southern Marion County

Belleview is a small city of about 5,400 people in southern Marion County. A post office opened in 1883, and the city incorporated in 1885. The name comes from the French for a beautiful view. US 27, US 301, and US 441 pass through the center of town and lead northwest about 10 miles to Ocala, the county seat. Lake Lillian and its historic district sit downtown, and the old Belleview School building is now City Hall.
