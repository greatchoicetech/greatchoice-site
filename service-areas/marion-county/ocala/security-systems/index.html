Questions before an Ocala assessment

Who installs security systems in Ocala, Florida?

Greatchoice Technologies does. The office is at 317 NE 36th Ave, Suite 3, and the install is scheduled from there. Cameras, door access, plate readers, gates, and intercoms are the equipment on this page.

Do the cameras need a monthly subscription?

Reolink can record with no monthly subscription. UniFi handles the video on the network, and a cloud subscription stays optional. The footage can remain at the property.

Can a phone open the door?

Yes. Touch Pass uses the phone at the reader. That same reader can also take a PIN, an NFC card, a fingerprint, a keyfob, or a face that has been enrolled.

How does a plate reader work at a gate?

The camera reads the plate, compares it with the approved list, and can open the gate. A visitor pass can expire by itself, and arrivals and departures are logged. The readers we install read plates on vehicles moving up to 45 mph, in daylight and at night.

Will this work with the gate operator already on the property?

Most operators already on a property will work with these gate systems. UL 325 entrapment protection is part of that setup, and an intercom can stand at the same entrance. More than one gate can be watched from a single screen.

Can the person at the door reach a phone?

Yes. The intercom shows the visitor, carries the conversation both ways, and can send the call to a mobile phone. Unlock remains on that screen and stays tied to the door system.

What happens after the install?

Cameras, the recorder, and door hardware can go on a visit schedule or on ongoing care. Maintenance or managed IT in Ocala is where those two are set side by side. Email, logins, and computers stay on the Ocala cybersecurity page.
