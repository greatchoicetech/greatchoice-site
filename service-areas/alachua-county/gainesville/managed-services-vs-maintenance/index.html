Proactive technology management

Managed IT

A continuing partnership that monitors, administers, secures, and guides the systems a Gainesville office depends on.

  • Monitoring of covered systems

  • Routine administration of covered technology

See if managed IT fits

Maintenance is planned care for the equipment that is covered. Managed IT takes a larger role in watching and administering that technology between visits. A repair, a subscription, or a security product is part of the plan only when the agreement lists it.

Maintenance plan levels

How often the preventative visit happens

Three levels for keeping covered hardware and infrastructure in working shape.

Foundation

Essential

For a steady setup that needs a professional checkup on a set schedule.

1 visit a year

Equipment checks, updates where they apply, priority remote help, and an on-site response target of 48 to 72 hours.

Enhanced protection

ProCare

For an office that wants the visit more often, plus endpoint protection.

2 visits a year

Everything in Essential, plus managed endpoint protection and an on-site response target of 24 to 48 hours.

Comprehensive maintenance

Total Secure

For the fullest visit schedule in this maintenance lineup.

Quarterly visits

ProCare's protection, plus a yearly network security review, a same-day on-site response target, and discounted parts and labor.

Those response times are targets. They do not promise the repair will be finished inside the window. The plan covers the equipment that was agreed. Price and any labor that comes with it are written into the agreement.

Common questions

Questions Gainesville owners ask about the two options

Who helps a Gainesville office choose between these two?

Greatchoice Technologies does, from the office at 317 NE 36th Ave, Suite 3. The five questions above are a starting point. The agreement is what names the covered equipment and the monthly work.

Does managed IT mean unlimited support?

Routine administration of the services that are covered sits in the monthly fee. Troubleshooting, corrective work, emergencies, and projects can be billed on their own under the agreement.

Does a maintenance plan include cybersecurity?

ProCare and Total Secure include managed endpoint protection. Managed IT adds day-to-day oversight of the security systems that agreement lists. Cameras and door hardware are on the Gainesville security systems page.

Can we start with maintenance and move to managed IT?

Yes. The scope can change when the office changes. Coverage and price are written down before the switch.

Are Microsoft 365, phones, and cloud backups included?

Only when the agreement names them. Licenses, hardware, and other vendor services are quoted separately. Microsoft 365 for Gainesville is on the cloud page, and phones are on the business phones page.

How is the price set?

Devices, locations, and risk are different at every office. What is included is written into the agreement for that environment. This page does not list a dollar amount.
