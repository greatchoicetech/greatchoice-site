Common questions

Questions law firms ask

How do you protect client files?

Access is limited to the people who need it. Email and computers are protected, and backups are part of managed support. We explain the scope in writing.

Can attorneys work away from the office?

Yes. Microsoft 365 and a secured laptop let someone open email and files from home or from a meeting. We set the accounts up and support them after.

Do you support the phones and email together?

Yes. The firm number, the handsets, and Microsoft 365 can be one relationship. Many number ports complete the next business day, and most finish within 72 hours.

What does ongoing support include?

Monitoring, updates, backups, and a help desk for the systems on the agreement. Month-to-month plans are available.
