Do you support dental offices?

Yes. Our team supports the computers, network, email, and phones a dental practice uses, from our office at 317 NE 36th Ave, Suite 3, Ocala.

We support the workstation, the storage, and the network your imaging software uses. Repair of the sensor or the dental unit stays with that equipment's vendor.

Yes. We sign a Business Associate Agreement when the practice requires one, and we include backups and access control in the plan.
