Common questions

Questions manufacturers ask

What do you support in a plant or warehouse?

Office computers, the Wi-Fi scanners and handhelds use, phones, backups, and the network those systems share. Equipment controls and machine repair stay with your equipment vendor.

Can warehouse scanners stay on Wi-Fi?

Yes. We design coverage for the aisles, the dock, and the yard, then test it with the scanner your crew already carries.

Can the office network stay separate from shop equipment?

Yes. When the shop equipment should sit on its own network, we set that separation up with you so the office and the machines do not share one open path.

Do you back up order and inventory files?

Yes, when those files are on systems in the agreement. Covered systems are monitored around the clock.
