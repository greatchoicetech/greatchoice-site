Common questions

Questions churches ask

Can you support the computers we use on Sunday?

Yes. We look after the presentation and streaming PCs, the Wi-Fi they use, and a way to reach them if something fails before the service.

Do you install cameras and door access?

Yes. Cameras, door readers, and video intercoms are part of our security work. We plan them for the rooms you want covered.

Can staff use Microsoft 365 during the week?

Yes. We set up and manage email, Teams, and shared files. Giving and membership software stays with the company that makes it. We support the computers it runs on.

Do you help with the church website?

Yes. We can build and look after a site that lists service times and a way to call. A 360° tour of the lobby is available when you want visitors to see the room first.
