Common questions

Questions contractors ask

Can you support the office and the job site?

Yes. The office computers, the shared files, and the phones the crew carries are one system. We come to the office or the yard when the work has to be hands-on.

Do you set up cameras at the yard?

Yes. Cameras, gates, door readers, and license plate recognition are part of our security work. We plan the layout for the property, then install it.

Can crews open files from a phone?

Yes, when the files are in Microsoft 365 or another shared place we set up. The same login works from the truck and from the desk.

What about business internet at the office?

We help choose and support the office connection, then look after the network behind it.
