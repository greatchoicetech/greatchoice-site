Common questions

Questions CPA firms ask

Can you support a firm through tax season?

Yes. We review the computers and network before the rush, then monitor covered systems while the office is busy.

How do you protect client tax data?

Access stays with the people who need the files. Backups and email protection are part of the plan we write down with you.

Do you work with the accounting software we already use?

Yes. We support the computers and network it runs on. Application updates stay with the software company. We coordinate a window when an update needs one.

What does a security review include?

Who can open the files, whether backups are current, and how email is protected. We explain what is working and what to change. It is a review of your setup, with no cost or obligation when you book the technology analysis.
