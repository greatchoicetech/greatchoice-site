Common questions

Questions nonprofits ask

Is managed IT realistic for a small staff?

Yes. The plan follows the computers and the data you have. Month-to-month plans are available, and the scope is written down.

How do you protect donor information?

We limit access to the computers, back up the files, and protect the email. The donor application itself stays with its vendor. We support the machines it runs on.

Do you support Microsoft 365?

Yes. We set up and manage the mailboxes, Teams, and files your staff use. We explain what each account is for before we add it.

Can you help with the website?

Yes. A clear site with your phone number, hours, and location is part of our digital marketing work. Churches can also see the church page.
