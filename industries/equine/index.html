On the property

Technology that has to leave the office

A desk in the farm office and a phone in the barn are one system. We plan the coverage for the buildings you actually use.

Wi-Fi past the office

Staff check schedules and cameras from the barn, the tack room, and the house. We place access points for those buildings, then test the walk between them.

Cameras and the gate

Cameras can watch the barn, the gate, the yard, and a stall you want on screen. License plate recognition and a gate controller are part of the security work when the property needs them.

Common questions

Questions horse farms ask

Can Wi-Fi reach the barn and the office?

Yes. We survey the buildings you use and place access points so the office, the barn, and the walk between them have coverage.

Do you install cameras and gate access?

Yes. Cameras, gates, door readers, and video intercoms are part of our security work. We can aim a camera at a barn, a gate, a yard, or a stall.

Can you support boarding or lesson software?

We support the computers, backups, and network that software runs on. Updates to the application itself stay with the company that makes it.
