Straight answers

Questions owners ask

How long does it take to switch?

Most businesses are fully live within two weeks. That includes number porting, configuration, mobile app setup, and staff training.

Can we keep our phone numbers?

Yes. Local and toll-free numbers transfer with no gap in service. In many cases the port starts the next business day, and most ports finish within 72 hours.

Can people use their cell phones?

Yes. The iPhone and Android app uses the business number. Personal calls and business calls stay separate, and nobody needs a second SIM.

Does this work with Microsoft Teams?

Yes. We deploy and manage Teams Phone so the phone system lives inside Teams, in the Microsoft 365 the office already uses. Licensing and the rest of Microsoft 365 are on the cloud page.

What happens if the internet goes down?

The connection fails over automatically, usually to LTE or 5G, so calls continue when the primary circuit fails. Both connections are monitored around the clock.

Do you set up conference rooms?

Yes. A room can include a 4K display, an auto-tracking camera, beamforming microphones, and one-touch join for Teams Rooms or Zoom Rooms.

How fast are everyday changes?

Extension moves, new users, auto-attendant updates, and routing changes are typically done the same day.

Do you run the cabling?

Yes. Cat6A and fiber are part of the phone project. The lines are labeled, tested, and documented, including as-built drawings.

How is it priced?

The phone service is a predictable monthly managed plan. Hardware, installation, and the ongoing management are itemized, so the bill is not a surprise. We will price it for this office in the technology analysis.
