Straight answers

Questions owners ask

What is managed IT, and how is it different from traditional IT support?

Managed IT is ongoing care. We monitor, maintain, and secure the technology so issues are caught before they stop the office. Traditional support, or a maintenance contract, is mostly reactive. You call when something is already broken.

What does the monitoring actually cover?

Servers, workstations, network devices, important applications, internet connections, and key background services, around the clock. That includes processor and memory load, storage, drive-failure warnings, and the firewall. A technician reviews the alerts, so a problem is often handled before anyone on the team notices it.

How fast do you respond when something goes wrong?

The goal is to resolve a reported problem inside four business hours. Most problems are handled remotely. When someone needs to be on site across Ocala and North Central Florida, our team can get there. How that differs from a maintenance visit is on Managed services versus a maintenance contract.

Do you handle Windows, Mac, and other devices?

Yes. We manage Windows, Mac, and Linux computers, plus servers, network equipment, and mobile devices. The security settings, updates, and policies stay consistent across them.

How do updates get applied?

We deploy operating system, application, firmware, and driver updates. Critical security patches are prioritized and often applied within 24 hours of release. Updates are tested so they do not interrupt the work, and we keep a record for compliance.

Do you test backups, or just run them?

We go past “backup completed.” Every month we perform a real recovery test, restoring systems and opening sample files, so there is written proof the data can come back. That includes Microsoft 365.

Can managed IT help with compliance?

Yes. The records from patching, log reviews, access reviews, and backup tests support requirements in HIPAA, the FTC Safeguards Rule, and similar frameworks. Cybersecurity is where the fuller compliance program lives.

What is the difference between managed services and a maintenance contract?

Managed IT watches covered systems and handles routine administration of what the agreement covers. A maintenance contract covers the equipment listed on that contract, with scheduled visits and an on-site window by tier. The tiers and the side-by-side are on Managed services versus a maintenance contract.

Do you only support remotely, or do you come on site?

Most issues are resolved remotely because that is faster. When the work has to be hands-on, local technicians come to the office throughout Ocala and North Central Florida.

How much does downtime actually cost?

Even a short outage adds up through lost productivity, delayed work, and frustrated staff. Use the estimator above. It multiplies the people affected, their hourly cost, how much of the work stops, and how long the outage lasts.
