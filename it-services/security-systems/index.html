Questions

Before the assessment

Do the cameras need a monthly subscription?

Reolink records without a monthly subscription. UniFi processes video on the network, and a cloud subscription is optional. The recording can stay on site.

Can a phone open the door?

Yes. Touch Pass uses the phone at the reader. The same system also accepts a PIN, an NFC card, a fingerprint, a keyfob, or an enrolled face.

How does a plate reader work at a gate?

The camera reads the plate, checks it against the approved list, and can open the gate. Visitor access can expire on its own, and each entry and exit is logged. The readers we install read plates on vehicles moving up to 45 mph, day or night.

Will this work with the gate operator already on the property?

The gate systems work with most existing operators. They include UL 325 entrapment protection, and an intercom can sit at the same entrance. Several gates can be watched from one screen.

Can the person at the door reach a phone?

Yes. A video intercom shows the visitor, carries a two-way conversation, and can forward the call to a mobile phone. Unlock stays on that same screen, tied to the door access system.

What happens after the install?

The cameras, recorder, and door hardware can be listed for scheduled upkeep or for ongoing care. Managed services and maintenance is the page that sorts those two. Email, logins, and the computers stay on the cybersecurity side.
