What we protect

One tool does not cover a business. These are the pieces we put in place, explained the way an owner would ask about them.

Past traditional antivirus

Endpoint protection

Security for every workstation, laptop, tablet, and server. It monitors how programs behave, so ransomware and unknown malware can be stopped before they spread.

  • Catches new attacks a signature list has not seen
  • Spots files being encrypted and can restore them

Before it reaches the inbox

Email protection

Phishing, spam, fake invoices, and dangerous links are stopped before anyone has to decide. Email is still the most common way attackers get in.

    The rules behind the work

    Standards we build around

    These are the frameworks clients and auditors actually ask about. We use them to decide what to fix first, and to keep the paperwork that proves it.

    A practical roadmap

    NIST Cybersecurity Framework

    Five jobs: know what you have, protect it, notice trouble, respond, and recover. We compare the current setup to those five and rank the fixes by business risk.

    Do the basics first

    CIS Controls

    A prioritized list of the steps that reduce the most risk. Every client starts with an inventory, a locked-down setup, multi-factor sign-in, and training. Regulated offices go further.

    Patient information

    HIPAA security requirements

    Medical, dental, and veterinary offices that store patient records have specific safeguards to meet. We sign a Business Associate Agreement and keep the evidence an audit expects: who can log in, what was accessed, and that data is encrypted.

    Accounting and tax firms

    FTC Safeguards Standard

    Firms that hold customer financial information need a written information security program, a named person in charge, multi-factor sign-in, and encryption. We help build that program and keep it current.

    Client confidentiality

    Rules for law firms

    ABA Model Rule 1.6(c), and the Florida Bar equivalent, require reasonable steps to protect client information. That means encrypted email or a client portal, multi-factor sign-in, and a plan for telling clients if something goes wrong.

    If you take cards

    PCI Data Security Standard

    Card brands require specific safeguards. We help pick the right self-check form, keep the payment system separate from the rest of the office, and turn on multi-factor sign-in for admin accounts.

    Microsoft 365

    Microsoft cloud security baseline

    Most small tenants never turn on the safer defaults. We work through Microsoft Secure Score, require multi-factor sign-in, block old login methods, and tighten email protection. Scores commonly move from the 35 to 50 percent range into 75 percent or higher within the first month.

    Do not trust the building

    Zero Trust

    Nobody gets in only because they are on the office network. Every request is checked for the person, the health of the device, and whether the login looks normal. We do this with Microsoft 365 and Cloudflare, without replacing the whole office.

    Straight answers

    Questions owners ask

    What cybersecurity services do you provide?

    Layered protection: behavior-based security on every computer, stronger email filtering, around-the-clock threat detection, Microsoft 365 sign-in controls, employee training, phishing practice, phone and tablet management, and, when you want them, cameras and door access.

    How is this different from regular antivirus?

    Traditional antivirus mostly looks for threats it has already seen. The protection we deploy also monitored behavior, so it can stop ransomware and unknown malware, isolate an infected computer, and help restore encrypted files.

    Why does email protection matter so much?

    Email is still the most common way into a business. We go past the filters included with Microsoft: phishing detection, defenses against fake invoices and impersonation, link scanning at the moment of the click, and a safe check of suspicious attachments.

    Do you monitor around the clock?

    Yes. The network, the computers, and the cloud systems are monitored continuously. A person reviews the alerts so a real threat is acted on, under a written response plan.

    What does identity and cloud security include?

    Multi-factor authentication on every account, rules based on location and device risk, tight control of admin access, and a look at apps the staff started using without telling anyone. The login is protected, not only the office firewall.

    Do you train employees?

    Yes. Short monthly lessons, about five to ten minutes, cover phishing and social engineering. Training can be shaped for the job, and we keep completion records for an audit. We also send practice phishing emails and coach anyone who clicks.

    Can you protect company phones?

    Yes. Through Microsoft Intune or Apple device management we set the rules, keep work apps separate from personal use, and can wipe company data if a phone is lost or stolen.

    Do you help with compliance frameworks?

    Yes. The program is built around the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, CIS Controls, the HIPAA Security Rule and Business Associate Agreements, the FTC Safeguards Rule for financial and CPA firms, ABA expectations for law firms, and PCI DSS where cards are accepted.

    What is Zero Trust, and where does Cloudflare fit?

    Zero Trust means a user or device is not trusted automatically. Every access request is checked. We use Cloudflare’s Zero Trust platform together with Microsoft 365 so a smaller business gets that model without building it from scratch.

    Is this only digital security?

    No. We also install cameras, with cloud recording and phone alerts, and electronic door access using keycards, fobs, or a phone. Access can change when someone is hired or leaves. The equipment is on the security systems page.

    Who is this for?

    Small and mid-sized businesses in Ocala and North Central Florida: medical and dental offices, law firms, accounting practices, shops, and other organizations that need practical security without enterprise cost or complexity.

    Book a cyber analysis. We review the current risks, name the gaps, and recommend a protection plan that fits the business and any compliance requirement you already have.
