The difference in the room

One agreement lists the gear. The other runs the office.

A maintenance visit is hands-on time with named equipment. Managed IT keeps watching after the technician leaves.

Maintenance contract

Scheduled care for the equipment on the list

Ongoing support and preventative maintenance for network hardware and installed equipment.

On site

Preventative visits

Each scheduled visit is a physical pass over the covered gear.

  • Visual and physical inspection, including wear, loose cabling, and the room around the rack
  • Cleaning of devices, racks, and connections
  • Cable inspection and testing
  • Power supplies, switches, and patch panels tested
  • Network performance and connectivity checked

During the visit

Health, updates, adjustments

  • System logs reviewed for errors and alerts
  • Hardware diagnostics on switches, routers, firewalls, and servers
  • Resource monitoring on models that support it
  • Backup verification when backup is part of the covered gear
  • Firmware and software updates when they apply, plus a security check when it applies
  • Camera aim, device placement, and help with a layout change

Beyond Routine Maintenance

A training session each year

Staff training is optional. Annual sessions can cover how the equipment is used, the monitoring tools, and everyday practice.

  • Priority remote support is included on every tier
  • On-site response uses the guaranteed window for the tier

Managed IT agreement

Routine administration, with someone watching

Managed IT is a fully proactive approach. We continuously monitor, maintain, and secure your entire technology environment so issues are caught and fixed before they disrupt your business. Traditional support (or a maintenance contract) is mostly reactive, you call when something breaks.
