Two ways to support the business

Both protect the technology already in place. The difference is how much day-to-day management Greatchoice handles.

Equipment-first protection

Preventative maintenance

Routine checkups, preventative maintenance, and priority help for the equipment listed in the agreement.

  • Scheduled physical equipment inspections
  • System health and performance checks
  • Firmware and software updates where they apply
  • Priority on-site and remote support, by tier
  • Recommendations that extend the life of the gear

Proactive technology management

Managed IT

An ongoing partnership to monitor, administer, secure, and guide the systems that keep the business running.

  • Monitoring of covered systems
  • Patch management and endpoint security
  • Routine administration of covered technology
  • Priority support by severity
  • Network security reviews and ongoing guidance
See if managed IT fits

Maintenance is planned care and service for covered equipment. Managed IT takes a more active role in the day-to-day oversight of covered technology. A repair, a subscription, or a security product is included when the agreement lists it.

Maintenance plan levels

How much preventative care

Three ways to keep the covered hardware and infrastructure in good condition.

Foundation

Essential

For a stable setup that needs periodic professional maintenance.

1 visit a year

Equipment checks, applicable updates, priority remote help, and a 48 to 72 hour on-site response target.

Enhanced protection

ProCare

For offices that need more frequent care and endpoint protection.

2 visits a year

Essential benefits, plus managed endpoint protection and a 24 to 48 hour on-site response target.

Comprehensive maintenance

Total Secure

For the fullest scheduled care in the maintenance lineup.

Quarterly visits

ProCare protection, plus an annual network security review, a same-day on-site response target, and discounted parts and labor.

Response times are targets. They are not a promise that the repair will be finished inside that window. Plans cover the agreed equipment. The price, and any labor included with it, is set in the agreement.

Common questions

A little more clarity

Does managed IT mean unlimited support?

Routine administration of covered services is included in the monthly fee. Troubleshooting, corrective work, emergencies, and projects can be billed separately under the agreement.

Does a maintenance plan include cybersecurity?

ProCare and Total Secure include managed endpoint protection. Managed IT adds ongoing oversight and administration of the security systems listed in that agreement.

Can we start with maintenance and move to managed IT?

Yes. The scope can change as the office changes. Coverage and price are agreed in writing.

Are Microsoft 365, phones, and cloud backups included?

They are included when the agreement lists them. Licenses, hardware, and other vendor services are quoted on their own.

How is the price set?

Each office has its own devices, locations, and risks. The price comes from that environment and the scope in the agreement.

What do managed services cost?

Our team analyzes all the equipment a business wants us to manage and cover in our monthly plan. Some businesses ask for everything and some choose a small number. The monthly cost will vary depending on what they want covered, and the best way is to get a free technology analysis.
